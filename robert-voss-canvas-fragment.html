About me

I’m the embedded tutor for this course and a STEM tutor at the Clovis Community College Tutorial Center. I graduated from CCC in Spring 2025 with degrees in Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, and Engineering-related coursework, and I’m also a computer science student at Fresno State.

If you get stuck, bring the problem, what you’ve tried, and a screenshot or photo if the issue is visual. That saves time, and it keeps the conversation pointed at the actual failure instead of the smoke around it.

What I help with
  • Circuit checks and wiring sanity checks
  • Debugging code or lab setups
  • Concept-to-application explanations
  • Homework guidance without doing the whole job for you
